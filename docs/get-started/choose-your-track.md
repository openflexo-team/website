# Choose your track

Once Openflexo runs, pick the path that matches what you want to do.

## I want to model and federate, without becoming a developer

Work through the [tutorials](/docs/guide/tutorials/first-free-model): from a first free model to a
diagram editor and a Petri net editor built without code. The
[cookbook](/docs/guide/cookbook/resource-centers) covers recurring tasks such as setting up a
resource center, and the [vocabulary](/docs/discover/vocabulary) explains the terms.

## I want to federate my own sources

Check first that a [technology adapter](/docs/develop/repository-map) exists for each of your
sources and how mature it is, then look at [what you can build](/docs/discover/what-you-can-build)
for the kinds of results to aim for. Federating sources means writing FML, the language of the
[User guide](/docs/category/user-guide).

## I want to develop, or contribute

Start with [setting up your workspace](/docs/develop/setup), then the
[repository map](/docs/develop/repository-map). To connect a new kind of source, read
[Write a technology adapter](/docs/develop/guides/write-a-technology-adapter). The
[community pages](/docs/community/get-involved) list the ways to contribute.

## I am a researcher

The [Research](/docs/research) section explains the scientific problem model federation answers and
lists the projects and publications.
