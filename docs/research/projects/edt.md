# Engineering Digital Twins (EDT)

Problem: Digital twins bring together models, simulation, data-driven techniques and AI across
software engineering, numerical analysis, HPC, distributed and embedded systems, and human-computer
interaction. Building them today still lacks shared scientific foundations, a common use-case
catalog, and a platform that federates the many tools and models involved.

Contribution: EDT is a French national research program, funded by France 2030, running for six
years from January 2026. It aims to establish the scientific foundations of digital twin
engineering, build an international scientific and technological community, maintain a public
catalog of digital twin engineering use cases, and deliver Artemis, a collaborative web-based
platform for engineering digital twins. The program is structured around five Focused Projects
(PC1-PC5) and five Cross-Cutting Actions (AT1-AT5). IMT Atlantique contributes in particular to
PC3, which develops methodologies, life cycles and domain-specific languages for engineering
digital twins, building on model federation work from [OpenFlexo](https://openflexo.org).
