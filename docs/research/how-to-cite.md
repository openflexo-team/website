# How to cite Openflexo

Cite the publication that matches what you are referring to. The BibTeX records are those exported
by [HAL](https://hal.science), where each paper can also be downloaded.

## The Openflexo platform and model federation in practice

[*10 years of Model Federation with Openflexo: Challenges and Lessons Learned*](https://hal.science/hal-04617492),
MODELS 2024 (Best Paper Award, Practice Track).

```bibtex
@inproceedings{bach:hal-04617492,
  TITLE = {{10 years of Model Federation with Openflexo: Challenges and Lessons Learned}},
  AUTHOR = {Bach, Jean-Christophe and Beugnard, Antoine and Champeau, Jo{\"e}l and Dagnat, Fabien and Gu{\'e}rin, Sylvain and Mart{\'i}nez, Salvador},
  URL = {https://hal.science/hal-04617492},
  NOTE = {Best Paper Award, Practice Track},
  BOOKTITLE = {{MODELS 2024: ACM/IEEE 27th International Conference on Model Driven Engineering Languages and Systems}},
  ADDRESS = {Linz, Austria},
  PUBLISHER = {{ACM}},
  PAGES = {12},
  YEAR = {2024},
  MONTH = Sep,
  DOI = {10.1145/3640310.3674084},
  KEYWORDS = {Abstraction ; modeling and modularity ; Interoperability ; Model management ; Experience report ; Model federation ; Model-driven software engineering ; Software and its engineering},
  PDF = {https://hal.science/hal-04617492v1/file/submitted_and_accepted_version-ModelFederationChallengesAndLessonsLearned.pdf},
  HAL_ID = {hal-04617492},
  HAL_VERSION = {v1},
}
```
