# MoM Challenge

The Model Management (MoM) workshop, co-located with MODELS, includes a Model Management
Challenge: the MoM community is invited to respond with submissions describing solutions
expressed in a technology of the authors' choice, emphasizing the merits and limitations of
their solution against the criteria defined in the challenge description. The challenge does not
aim to be a competition but a forum to gather stakeholders in model management and let them
discuss their approach on a level playing field.

## 1. The 2025 challenge: satellite configuration

[MoM 2025](https://mom2025.wp.imt.fr/mom-challenge/) (co-located with MODELS 2025) set the
inaugural challenge on model management during the configuration of a satellite: a set of
artefacts in multiple formalisms, the links between them, and a number of scenarios describing
changes and how a model management solution should propagate them. The challenge contents are
[on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15285132), and its public git repository is
[mom-challenge/satellite-config](https://github.com/mom-challenge/satellite-config).

## 2. Our contribution

We responded to the challenge with [OpenFlexo](https://openflexo.org), showing how it federates
the challenge's heterogeneous models — OWL ontologies, JSON architecture files, Excel-based
requirements, and text reports — while keeping traceable and consistent links between them, and
evaluated our contribution against the five key scenarios identified in the challenge.

Hiba Hnaini, Chahrazed Boudjemila, Sylvain Guerin. *Openflexo as a Model Management Platform for
the MoM 2025 Satellite Configuration Challenge*. In the Proceedings of the 2025 ACM/IEEE 28th
International Conference on Model Driven Engineering Languages and Systems Companion
(MODELS-C), pp. 732-741, IEEE, Grand Rapids, MI, United States, 2025.

[Read the paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11273234) — see also the
[publications](/docs/research/publications) page.
